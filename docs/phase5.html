Mobile Device Management (MDM)

App Protection Policies applied via Intune to all employee mobile devices (iOS & Android) to protect corporate data.

Microsoft Authenticator Setup

How to set up Microsoft Authenticator

  • Step 1: Download Microsoft Authenticator from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android).
  • Step 2: Open the app and tap Add account → Work or school account.
  • Step 3: Sign in with your company email and password.
  • Step 4: Approve the on-screen prompt — your account is now protected with MFA.
